## Summary

Replace the hand-committed browser wheels with a derived, gitignored two-wheel build (`pylabrobot` from the submodule + a new `praxis_browser` wheel carrying praxis's own browser source), keyed by a single generated `manifest.json` that is the only place a filename or version appears in hand-written source. Convert the bootstrap's seven silent `try/except -> console.warn` regions into a staged, fail-closed boot that raises in the cell, with a build-SHA equality assertion plus a shell-injected superproject-SHA comparison as the drift detectors.
